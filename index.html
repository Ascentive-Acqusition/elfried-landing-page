"Attention is rented. Trust is earned. And trust compounds." Hi, I'm Elf. I build communities for a living. Marketing is my ingredient of choice, and I think most people are using it wrong. Not the tools. The thinking and the systems behind them.

Most people are fighting the wrong battle. Performance vs brand. Paid vs organic. Content vs community. The brands that win aren't the ones that picked the right channel. They're the ones who make everything work together.

After 15+ years working with some of the world's most culturally significant brands, I keep coming back to the same truth: the only thing that compounds is trust. And that trust becomes a defensive moat — the thing that protects your brand when the storm comes.

From scaling Gymshark from 1.5M to 20M+ followers and contributing to a $1.7B valuation, to advising global enterprise giants — the lesson never changes. Community isn't a marketing channel. It's the strongest growth advantage on Earth and, more importantly, the strongest defence a brand can build. Create real affinity with people, and they don't just buy. They protect you.

Today, as Co-Founder & CEO of Butterfly Effect, I help leadership teams turn community into a commercial lever. A system. Because attention is rented. Trust is earned. And that trust isn't just a growth asset — it's insurance.

In 2024, Favikon ranked me the second most influential LinkedIn creator globally. 415K followers. 500M+ impressions. 9M engagements.

Philosophy

Two Lenses.
One System.

I approach community through two distinct lenses to ensure growth is both cultural and commercial.

Ecosystems

Conversation drives commerce. Our goal is to ensure more people talk about you than you talking at them. We design the environment where your brand and your people coexist — the strategy, the narrative, and the behavioural science that turns a customer into an advocate.

Infrastructure

We don't just "run social." We give community control back to the brand by obsessing over the process of execution — building the systems, the processes, and the data-driven foundations that make community a reliable commercial lever.
